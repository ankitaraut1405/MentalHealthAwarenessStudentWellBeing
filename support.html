Student Mental Health Support

Support and communication can be important parts of student well-being. Students can consider reaching out to trusted people when they are facing difficulties.

Talk to Someone

Students can talk with a trusted friend, family member, teacher, mentor, or another person they feel comfortable communicating with.

College Support

Colleges may provide teachers, mentors, counsellors, or student-support services that students can approach.

Listen and Support

Supporting another student can begin with listening respectfully and taking their concerns seriously.

Open Communication

A supportive environment can make it easier for students to communicate their concerns and experiences.

Professional Support

A qualified mental-health professional can provide appropriate support when someone is experiencing mental-health difficulties.

Support Matters

Knowing that support is available can make starting a conversation about well-being easier.
