Mental Health Awareness

Mental health is an important part of overall well-being. Understanding mental health can help students become more aware of their emotional and psychological well-being.

What is Mental Health?

Mental health relates to emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It can influence how people think, feel, communicate, and respond to everyday life.

Students and Mental Health

Students may experience challenges connected with studies, academic pressure, relationships, responsibilities, sleep, and daily life.

Why Awareness Matters

Awareness can encourage open conversations, reduce misunderstanding, and help students understand where support may be available.
